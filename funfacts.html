Did you know?

The Sun

The Sun is a massive, glowing star at the center of our solar system which provides light, heat, and energy that sustain life on Earth and drive planetary orbits and weather systems

The Solar System

The solar system consists of the Sun and all objects orbiting it inclusive of the eight planets, the moon, asteroids and meteroids forming a dynamic system shaped by gravity and cosmic forces

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun, supporting life with its atmosphere, water, and ecosystems, featuring land, oceans, and climate systems, while orbiting the Sun and rotating daily


Space is Silent

Space is silent because it lacks any medium to carry sound waves making it a vacuum yet it still produce electromagnetic signals detectable by instruments

Moon Footprints Last

Moon footprints last because the Moon has no atmosphere so nothing erodes them allowing prints left by astronauts to remain visible for millions of years

Astronauts grow taller

Astronauts grow taller in space because microgravity reduces compression on the spine, allowing vertebrae to expand slightly, temporarily increasing height

Saturn can Float

Saturn floats because it is mostly made of light gases like hydrogen and helium, giving it a low average density lower than water, making it buoyant if placed in a giant ocean


Black holes have a strong gravity

Black holes have strong gravity because their mass is extremely concentrated in a tiny space, warping space-time so intensely that nothing, not even light, can escape their intense gravitational pull

The milky way

The Milky Way is a spiral galaxy containing billions of stars, planets, and cosmic dust, including our solar system, with a central black hole and vast regions of gas, light, and dark matter

Venus spins backwards

Venus spins backward, or retrograde rotation, likely due to massive collisions during its early formation, causing it to rotate opposite most planets, making its Sun rise in the west and set in the east


Rocket

The first rockets were developed by the Chinesse using gun powder operating the Newtons third law of motion whereby expelled gases causes an upward thrust

Space Shuttle

Space shuttle is the first space craft to be re-used by NASA.It has a relatively high speed compared to the rocket and are also resistant to adverse temperatures

Space Ship

A space ship has a 360 degrees window for observing earth and it is engineered for extreme conditions.It also has bathroom logistics for the astronauts in sapce

Space Probe

Space probe is an unmanned space craft designed to travel beyond the orbit of earth t study and explore celestial beings by capturing photos ot them
